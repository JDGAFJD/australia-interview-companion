PREPARATION CHECKLIST

Interview day

A short checklist to rehearse before the Zoom interview. Your answers should match your own documents and application.

Use this for preparation before the call. Your consultant advised a clear desk with no helping material during the interview.

Set up the call

  • Use a laptop in a quiet, well-lit room and check your camera, microphone and Zoom connection.
  • Wear a neat collared shirt, look presentable and keep your passport close for identity checks.
  • Close notes and other helping material before the interview begins, following your consultant's advice.

Rehearse the study answers

  • Give a clear introduction, then explain why Murdoch University and why this course in Australia rather than a comparable option in Pakistan.
  • Know the course name, key modules and how they connect to your software engineering and automation background.
  • Check the offer and CoE for total tuition, what has already been paid, what remains, how it will be paid and the exact scholarship percentage. The guide mentions 15%; verify it against your current offer.
  • Know your living-cost plan. Home Affairs currently lists AUD 29,710 for a student's 12-month living-cost evidence, separate from tuition and travel.

Review the sponsor affidavit

  • Know your father's two stated annual income sources: AMZ Aluminiums and agricultural land, plus the total annual income and the date-specific bank balance.
  • Rehearse which assets belong to your father, which belong to you and which plot is jointly owned with your sister. Use the affidavit's exact measurements and values if asked.
  • Your consultant mentioned land in Gujranwala, but the affidavit itself does not give the land's location. Confirm the location from the property papers before stating it.
  • Explain that your father is sponsoring tuition and living expenses, and distinguish available bank funds from property values.

Current work and verification

  • Start with your current Automation Engineer role at Nomad Internet. Explain your actual automation work using n8n, APIs and webhooks in plain language.
  • If asked about earlier work, answer accurately. Do not volunteer unrelated details, but do not hide or change your employment history.
  • Be ready to explain your weekly salary, the currency conversion and how the deposits appear in your bank statement. Use your own payslips and records for the exact amount.
  • If employment verification comes up, explain the official employer email process only as you can genuinely confirm it. Ask the consultant to clarify the phrase heard as “climate verification” before the call.
